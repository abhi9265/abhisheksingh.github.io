SYSTEM DESIGN MINDSET

How I reason about production systems.

I start with the data contract and failure modes, then design the happy path. Reliability is a system property, not a post-launch patch.

01

Design for failure

Retries, idempotency, replay safety, state transitions, and explicit unknown outcomes belong in the design.

02

Protect the data

Validate early, quarantine bad records, enforce contracts, and preserve lineage.

03

Prove behavior

Tests, smoke checks, benchmarks, runbooks, and evidence matter more than unverified claims.

04

Use AI carefully

Deterministic logic first; AI where ambiguity genuinely benefits from reasoning and human review.
